# Concept C — 鲸尾信标 / Tail Beacon

```text
Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: minimal native desktop application icon concept for macOS and Windows
Primary request: redesign the ImagePorter / 鲸舟 Tail Beacon mark as one compact near-square connected cyan transfer symbol with one large fully enclosed deep-navy whale-tail negative-space cutout and exactly one bold integrated rightward arrow
Subject: one unified rounded geometric transfer tile/shield whose outer right edge forms a distinct arrowhead; visible symbol width-to-height ratio must be between 0.9 and 1.2, ideally 1.05; center a fully enclosed whale-tail cutout in the upper-middle, with two broad symmetrical flukes, a deep central V, and a short broad peduncle, surrounded on all sides including below by a thick continuous cyan border; the tail must occupy about one third of the symbol and remain unmistakable at actual 16 pixels
Style/medium: strict flat vector-friendly geometry, hard clean edges, minimal, professional developer-tool character, no more than three major shapes total, designed first on a 16-pixel grid
Composition/framing: centered single connected symbol, 16 percent safe-area padding on every side, near-square rather than horizontal, bold arrowhead at least one quarter of symbol width, thick positive and negative strokes that survive 16, 24, and 32 pixels
Color palette: solid deep navy background and one solid ice-blue/cyan symbol only, uniform flat fills
Constraints: whale tail must be a fully enclosed deep-navy hole with cyan beneath its peduncle and no opening to the exterior; exactly one rightward arrow and one arrowhead; no separate vertical bar, no stem-and-loop construction, no P-shaped curve, no readable I or P, no letters, no detached components, no mascot, no mockup, no label, no watermark, no tiny details, no thin outlines, no gradients, no glow, no shadow, no bevel, no texture, do not imitate the Docker whale trademark
```
